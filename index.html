    Case study2026

    Customer Churn Analysis

    Data & business analysis · IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (Kaggle)

    7,043customers analyzed
    26.5%overall churn rate
    6Power BI dashboards

    The question

    Which telecom customers leave, why do they leave, and what should the business do first to keep them?

    Data analysis

    • Cleaned and prepared the customer data in Excel and Power BI.
    • Built DAX measures for Churn Rate, Retention Rate, Revenue Loss, CLTV, ARPU, and customer risk metrics.
    • Designed 6 interactive Power BI dashboards: Executive Overview, Service Analysis, Customer Segmentation, Revenue Analysis, High-Risk Customer Analysis, and Strategic Recommendations.

    Business analysis

    • Ran stakeholder analysis and gathered functional and non-functional requirements.
    • Wrote user stories with acceptance criteria and prioritized them with MoSCoW.
    • Planned the work with Agile/Scrum and ran risk analysis in Jira.

    What I found

    Churn rate by contract type0–50%
    Month-to-month42.7%
    One year11.3%
    Two year2.8%
    • Overall churn was 26.5%: 1,869 of 7,043 customers left.
    • Month-to-month customers churned at 42.7%, compared with 11.3% on one-year and 2.8% on two-year contracts.
    • Further churn patterns showed up across tenure, internet service, payment method, tech support, and online security.

    What I delivered

    • An executive-level recommendation framework with priorities, implementation timelines, business owners, and expected business impact, focused on customer retention and revenue protection.
    • Actionable retention recommendations tied to the churn drivers above.

    Customer Onboarding & KYC Process Improvement

    Data & business analysis · Python, Power BI, DAX, Excel, Jira

    2M+applications analyzed
    3Power BI dashboards
    6dimensions analyzed

    The question

    What drives KYC approval outcomes, and where does the onboarding process need to change?

    Data analysis

    • Analyzed 2M+ customer onboarding applications in Python across channel, KYC tier, state, applicant age, verification scores, and completion time.
    • Cleaned the data and ran exploratory analysis and statistical testing to identify patterns and assess potential factors associated with approval outcomes.
    • Built 3 interactive Power BI dashboards with DAX KPIs covering executive KYC performance, tier-level performance, geographic analysis, application volume, approval rates, verification metrics, and completion time.

    Business analysis

    • Defined business requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and MoSCoW priorities based on the process gaps found.
    • Created a Business Requirements Document (BRD).
    • Designed As-Is and To-Be onboarding processes and wireframes.
    • Managed requirements in Jira, organizing user stories into epics, sprints, priorities, and story points.

    What I recommended

    Onboarding improvements focused on four areas:

    • Failure guidance
    • Retry handling
    • Status visibility
    • Stage-level monitoring
